# Engineering Assumptions & Open-Issues Register

Unresolved site-specific or equipment-specific engineering parameters must never be resolved by silent assumption. Every open item is represented as a configurable parameter or explicit interface boundary until validated against approved vendor and utility engineering documents.

| ID | Category | Engineering Open Issue | Configurable Software Representation | Validation Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **OI-001** | Biomass | Equipment nameplate rating vs continuous operating max, minimum stable load, ramp rates (kW/h), startup/shutdown durations, and min up/down times must be confirmed per project. | `BiomassOperationalConfig`: `nameplate_kw`, `operating_max_kw`, `min_load_kw`, `ramp_up_kw_per_hour`, `ramp_down_kw_per_hour`, `startup_time_seconds`, `shutdown_time_seconds`, `min_up_time_seconds`, `min_down_time_seconds`. | Biomass OEM / EPC | Open |
| **OI-002** | Solar PV | PV array DC kWp vs actual AC inverter export ceiling, power factor envelope, and active curtailment interface must be confirmed against vendor datasheets. | `PVOperationalConfig`: `nameplate_kwp`, `rated_ac_kw`, `curtailment_enabled`, `available`. | PV Inverter Vendor / EPC | Open |
| **OI-003** | BESS | Usable energy capacity, PCS active/reactive rating, round-trip charge/discharge efficiencies, hard physical SoC bounds, and required islanding reserve SoC need vendor confirmation. | `BESSOperationalConfig`: `nominal_capacity_kwh`, `effective_capacity_kwh`, `max_charge_kw`, `max_discharge_kw`, `soc_min_fraction`, `reserve_soc_fraction`, `soc_max_fraction`, efficiencies. | BESS / PCS Vendor | Open |
| **OI-004** | Grid-Forming | Role allocation during islanded operation (BESS PCS grid-forming master vs biomass isochronous/droop sharing) and black-start support are site-specific. | Modeled as supervisory active-power balance in Phases 1 to 4; fast V/f loops remain outside software. | Protection & PCS Engineer | Open |
| **OI-005** | Outage Detection | Exact voltage/frequency trip criteria and duration threshold separating a momentary outage from a prolonged outage require protection engineering sign-off. | `grid_connected` status input + configurable `momentary_outage_threshold_seconds`. | Utility / Protection Engineer | Open |
| **OI-006** | Switchgear | PCC Breaker behavior during momentary outages and breaker open/close command ownership (Utility SCADA vs local relay vs EMS) must be formally assigned. | Controller never commands physical breakers directly; Module 2/6 consume status and permissives only. | Utility SCADA / EPC | Open |
| **OI-007** | Synchronization | Exact `sync_permissive` interlock signals (synchrocheck relay, voltage/frequency/phase windows) and reconnection load-transfer ramp rates need definition. | Modeled via `sync_permissive`, `operator_ack_restoration`, and simulated sequence steps in Module 6. | Commissioning Lead | Open |
| **OI-008** | Islanded Feeder | Target islanded feeder demand profile, peak/minimum load, critical load tiers, and minimum reserve duration are project-specific. | `local_load_kw` time series input + explicit `unmet_load_kw` reporting + configurable `reserve_soc_fraction`. | Distribution Study Lead | Open |
| **OI-009** | Parasitic Load | Plant auxiliary/parasitic load shedding rules when total off-grid available generation plus BESS cannot cover `parasitic_load_kw`. | Treated as mandatory station auxiliary load; if unservable, Module 5 flags `infeasible` and `STATION_AUX_DEFICIT`. | Plant Operations Engineer | Open |
| **OI-010** | Biomass Maintenance | Exact mechanical/electrical interlocks for maintenance mode entry/exit and whether off-grid PV+BESS operation is allowed during maintenance. | `biomass_maintenance_mode` input forces `biomass_command_kw = 0.0` in Module 5 across all operating modes. | Plant Operations / Biomass OEM | Open |
| **OI-011** | SCADA Protocols | Field protocols (Modbus TCP/RTU, OPC UA, IEC 60870-5-104), point lists, scaling factors, and External SCADA tags are not yet specified. | Deferred strictly to Phase 5 `adapters/`; `core/` remains 100% protocol-agnostic. | SCADA Integrator | Open |
| **OI-012** | Telemetry Health | Scan rate, timestamp authority, stale-data timeout threshold, and fail-safe action on communication loss (hold last valid vs ramp to safe state). | Configurable `max_telemetry_age_seconds` and `comm_loss_action` (`"emergency_fault"` or `"hold_safe"`). | SCADA Integrator / Safety Lead | Open |
| **OI-013** | Control Ownership | Local/Remote and Manual/Auto transfer hierarchy between External SCADA, operator HMI, and Hybrid EMS. | Explicit operator acknowledgment flags and `hardware_commands_enabled = false` in Phases 0 to 4. | Plant Operations / Utility | Open |
| **OI-014** | Reactive Power & V/f | Reactive power (kVAR), feeder voltage regulation, and frequency droop envelopes are out of scope for active-power dispatch Phases 1 to 4. | Deferred to Phase 6 HIL review; power fields use explicit `_kw` suffixes so `_kvar` fields can be added non-destructively later. | Grid Interconnection Engineer | Open |
